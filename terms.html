What Still Rolling is

Still Rolling is a private tool run by the owner of the Still Rolling channels. It edits short highlight videos of public livestreams and uploads them to social media accounts that the owner controls and has connected to the tool. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Connected accounts

An account is connected only when its owner signs in through the platform's official login (for example "Log in with TikTok") and grants permission. Still Rolling uses that permission only to upload videos and read the basic profile details needed to show which account is connected. The permission can be removed at any time in the platform's settings.

Content

Videos are made from livestreams of creators who allow their streams to be clipped, and every video credits the original creator. Content that a creator asks to remove is removed. The owner of the connected account is responsible for what is published on it, and every post follows the rules of the platform it is posted on.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What we collect

When the owner connects one of their accounts (TikTok or YouTube), Still Rolling receives an access token and the account's basic profile information (display name, avatar, account ID). Nothing else is collected. Still Rolling has no visitors' accounts, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and this website sets no cookies.

How it is used

The token is used only to upload videos to that same account and to check the status of those uploads. Profile information is used only to show which account is connected.

Where it is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to anyone else, sold or shared.

Removing access

Disconnecting an account in Still Rolling deletes its token. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and services).
